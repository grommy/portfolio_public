# Daily move of a personal portfolio

One page, rewritten after each trading day: how the book moved, by sleeve and by line.

- Nothing of the holding beyond sleeve weights: no money amounts, no quantities, no weight of a single line.
- A line's card shows its public price chart and the company's public figures.
- Only the latest day is kept: the page replaces the one before it, in the history too.
- Inside a sleeve the lines are listed from the largest to the smallest.
- A personal learning project. Nothing here is investment advice.

`index.html` is generated; do not edit it by hand.

## Growth regime pages

`research/` holds one page per company: reported revenue and EPS growth joined to analyst consensus, with a stated rule for where the growth regime breaks. Each page reads in English and Ukrainian.

- Company figures only: revenue, EPS and analyst estimates. Nothing about the portfolio.
- The pages are generated; do not edit them by hand.
